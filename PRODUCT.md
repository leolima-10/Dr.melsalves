# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

React with Vite and Tailwind CSS; static deployment to Vercel.

## Users

Prospective patients arriving mainly from Dra. Mel Salves' Instagram link, often on a phone, looking for information and a way to request an evaluation.

## Product Purpose

A one-page professional website that presents confirmed information about Dra. Mel Salves' facial harmonization and advanced aesthetics work and guides visitors to contact her on WhatsApp to request an evaluation.

## Positioning

The site foregrounds an interactive before-and-after comparison and frames care around naturalness, balance, and individual evaluation.

## Operating Context

Visitors may use an in-app mobile browser. Primary conversion is a contextual WhatsApp conversation; Instagram is a secondary destination.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One responsive landing page with an accessible draggable comparison slider, result filters, mobile navigation, FAQ, and WhatsApp links.
- No forms, trackers, cookies, or unsupported treatment claims.
- Only Harmonização Facial, Preenchimento Labial, and Estética Avançada are confirmed services.
- Use React, Vite, and Tailwind CSS; deploy as a static site to Vercel.
- Do not assume a city, address, hours, education, registration, years of experience, or patient testimonials.
- Respect keyboard interaction, touch target size, visible focus, and reduced-motion preferences.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Dra. Mel Salves.
- Confirmed bio: specialist in Harmonização Facial, expert in Preenchimento Labial, and professor at Rede Pro Cursos.
- Voice: elegant, welcoming, safe, natural, and concise in Brazilian Portuguese.
- Visual direction approved for planning: warm honey/champagne palette, refined editorial typography, subtle honeycomb geometry, and understated motion.

## Product Principles

- Make individual evaluation, not guaranteed outcomes, the basis for care.
- Use only verified professional and patient information.
- Keep the comparison interaction usable with touch, mouse, and keyboard.
- Make WhatsApp contact clear and contextual across the page.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, accessible navigation and controls, keyboard-operable comparison sliders, visible focus, reduced-motion support, readable contrast, and mobile-friendly touch targets.
