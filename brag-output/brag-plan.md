# Brag plan: Dra. Mel Salves (site)

## What it is
A one-page site (pt-BR) for Dra. Mel Salves, a *biomédica esteta* doing facial harmonization in Salvador and the surrounding region. People who arrive from her Instagram, mostly on a phone, can compare before/after results by dragging a slider. Each procedure then opens a WhatsApp chat with the message already written.

- **For whom:** prospective patients tapping the link in her Instagram bio, usually inside the in-app browser.
- **What sets it apart:** the before/after slider comes right after the hero. Every WhatsApp button pre-writes the right message ("Olá, Dra. Mel! Quero saber mais sobre harmonização facial."). It uses an editorial honey/espresso look with honeycomb geometry.
- **Most impressive claim:** from the Instagram bio to a ready-to-send WhatsApp message that already names the procedure.
- **Visual hook:** the real hero, with "Seu rosto é sua *identidade.*" in Cormorant over her office photo.
- **Share caption:** see `share-copy.txt`.

## Angle
**From the Instagram bio to the right conversation.** The video shows the real site doing its job on a phone: it lands, you compare results, you tap "Conversar" and the message is already written.

## Tone
`polished`: serious, elegant and restrained. It has long holds, soft fades and staggered exits, with no hard flashes. All copy on screen is pt-BR, matching the product.

## Visual identity (taken from `src/styles.css`)
- Colors: cream `#fbf6ee`, sand `#f4eadb`, champagne `#f1e4cf`, honey `#c9973f`, light honey `#e5c27e`, espresso `#2a1d14`, muted `#68594c`, WhatsApp green `#236d4b`.
- Type: Cormorant Garamond 500 for display (italic for accents), DM Sans for body. Both are the project's self-hosted woff2 files.
- Motifs: the honeycomb comes from `buildHoneycomb()` (`src/lib/honeycomb.js`, the real generator), plus the bee logo stroke drawing (`BrandLogo` markup).
- **Real UI:** the actual site runs on the Vite dev server inside iframes: desktop for the hook, 390-px mobile inside a phone for the demo. The project's own animations are switched off with reduced-motion emulation. The video drives every state as a pure function of time (scroll position, the slider's `--pos`, inline transforms).

## Privacy and stand-ins
- **Patient before/after photos are NOT used.** Publication authorization is still pending (README), and these are real people's health images. In the phone, the first slider's images are swapped for a brand stand-in: an empty honeycomb ("Antes") vs a honey-filled honeycomb ("Depois"). The other result cards are hidden so no patient photo scrolls past. That card's caption is relabeled "Imagem ilustrativa / Como funciona o comparador" so it doesn't claim to be a lip case.
- The hero portrait is Dra. Mel herself, the brand's public face on her own site. It is kept, and this is flagged to the user.
- Nothing else is personal. The prefilled WhatsApp text is generated by `getWhatsAppLink()`. The URL shown is `SITE_URL`.

## Music
96 BPM (beat = 0.625 s, 32 beats = 20.0 s), F major, warm and minimal: soft electric-piano chords (Fmaj7 · Am7 · B♭maj7 · C6/9), sub bass, soft kick on 1 and 3, a quiet off-beat shaker, and a glassy pluck motif. Effects are tuned to the key and quantized to the same grid. The mix runs effects under the music, reverb shared with the music, and a master limiter.
